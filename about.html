Rajesh Tracker

A daily companion for japam, habits and spiritual practice. Works fully offline on Android.

What it does

  • Japams: daily counts against daily, weekly, monthly and yearly targets, with lifetime totals.
  • Habits: yes/no and timed habits, on chosen days of the week.
  • Time: timers, sessions and moods through the day.
  • Day notes: moments worth remembering, with "On this day" and yearly reminders.
  • To-do: tasks with due dates, reminders and a progress log.
  • Fast: Ekadasi and Amavasya calendar with fasting reminders.
  • Temple: visits, darshan and mokku (vows) with their status.
  • History: day, week, month and year summaries.

Your data stays yours

Everything is stored on your phone. If you want, the app backs up to a private folder in your own Google Drive, and you can restore from it on any phone. No ads, no analytics, no accounts.
