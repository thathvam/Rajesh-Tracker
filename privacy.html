Rajesh Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

In short

Rajesh Tracker keeps your data on your phone. It has no servers of its own, shows no ads, has no analytics and never sells or shares your data. If you turn on Google Drive backup, copies go only to your own Google Drive, in a private folder that only this app can use.

What the app stores

What you enter in the app: japam counts and targets, habits, time sessions and moods, day notes, to-dos, fasting days and temple visits. All of it is kept in a database on your phone.

The app keeps a daily copy of that database on the phone for the last 7 days, so a mistake can be undone.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you tap Turn on under Backup, the app asks Google for one permission: drive.appdata. It lets the app store its own files in a hidden folder in your Google Drive.

  • The app can't see, open or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Backups are sent straight from your phone to Google. They never pass through anyone else.
  • Google stores them under its own privacy policy. They count towards your Drive storage.

Rajesh Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Files you choose

When you save a backup file, restore one, or import an export from your Google Sheet, you pick the file yourself in Android's file picker. The app reads or writes only that file.

Phone permissions

  • Notifications, alarms, start after reboot: for the reminders you set (to-dos, fasting, yearly day notes). They are created on the phone.
  • Internet: used only for Google Drive backup and restore.

Deleting your data

  • On the phone: uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored there.
  • In Google Drive: open Google Drive on the web, go to ⚙ Settings › Manage apps, find Rajesh Tracker, click Options › Delete hidden app data. To also remove the app's access, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/thathvam/Rajesh-Tracker/issues.
